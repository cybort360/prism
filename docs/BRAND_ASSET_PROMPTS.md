# Prism brand and onboarding asset prompts

All assets were produced with the built-in image generator. The supplied onboarding screenshot was used as a mood and material reference only. The new compositions and logo geometry are original.

## Prism logo mark

Create one compact, vertically oriented faceted prism emblem with five or six polygonal facets converging around a small negative-space core. Keep the silhouette balanced, symmetrical, and readable at small header size. Use deep teal, mint, and pale aqua highlights with restrained translucent glass depth. Center the mark on a genuinely transparent square canvas with generous padding. Include no text, letters, container, border, outer shadow, watermark, thin lines, or cryptocurrency symbols.

## Person-to-person payments

Create a premium 3D illustration with a transparent glass orbital sculpture rising from a small oval pedestal. Place two friendly, diverse, stylized head-and-shoulder avatars in circular glass medallions at opposite ends. Put a mint glass token with the Prism mark at the center. Add two or three small glass bubbles. Use polished editorial 3D rendering, soft white studio light, clear glass tubes, frosted mint acrylic, and a centered composition suited to a 340 by 250 point mobile slot. Include no text, numbers, cards, watermark, or cryptocurrency symbols.

## Contacts

Create three friendly, diverse, stylized 3D head-and-shoulder avatars inside clear rounded glass contact bubbles. Connect the bubbles to a small central Prism token with fine translucent mint arcs. Add two subtle glass beads. Use a compact triangular composition, soft white studio lighting, transparent glass rings, and matte character surfaces. Use a genuinely transparent background with no text, phone interface, watermark, or hard outlines.

## Escrow

Create a clear glass escrow capsule on a low oval pedestal. Inside, place a mint glass coin carrying the Prism mark. Wrap a small translucent clock ring around the capsule and add two tiny glass beads. Use a simple centered composition, soft white studio lighting, clear glass, frosted mint acrylic, and genuine transparency. Include no text, numbers, padlock, watermark, dark scene, or bank-vault styling.

## Passkey

Create a slim translucent glass phone at a slight three-quarter angle on a clear pedestal. Put the Prism mark on the screen inside a soft biometric halo formed by four rounded corner brackets. Add one small glass key-shaped light trail and two tiny orbiting bubbles. Use a centered, portrait-friendly composition, soft white studio lighting, mint and teal glass materials, and genuine transparency. Include no text, numbers, app interface, Apple logo, watermark, or copied Face ID symbol.

## App icon

Create a 1024 by 1024 opaque app icon around the Prism mark. Use a full-bleed deep forest teal background with a subtle mint radial glow. Center the mark at roughly 52 percent of the canvas. Preserve breathing room and crisp small-size readability. Include no text, letters, circle container, border, watermark, transparent pixels, or external shadow.
